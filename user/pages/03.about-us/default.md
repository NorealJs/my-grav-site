    <div>
      <p class="about-eyebrow">Our story</p>
      <h2 id="story-title">From shared curiosity to a campus community.</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="about-prose">
      <p>Founded in 2019, Tech Innovators Society brings students together around a shared interest in technology. What matters most is not how much experience you arrive with, but your willingness to ask questions, try things, and learn alongside others.</p>
      <p>Today, our community includes 85 active members and hosts a range of activities throughout the year. We aim to make technology more approachable through peer learning, practical projects, and opportunities to contribute.</p>
    </div>
  </section>
